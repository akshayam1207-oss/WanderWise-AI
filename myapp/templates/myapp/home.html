GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions

Is WanderWise AI free to use?

Yes. Enter your trip details and get a full plan with no sign-up.

Are the budget numbers exact?

No. They are estimates based on your travel mode and stay style. Always check current prices before booking.

Can I plan a trip to any place?

Yes. Popular places like Ooty, Manali, Goa, Bali, Munnar and Jaipur have curated attractions. Other places get general suggestions you can open in Google Maps.

Can I save my itinerary?

Use the Print / Save as PDF button or the Copy itinerary button on your results page.
